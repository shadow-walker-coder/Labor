Macro Monitor

A running check on the U.S. economy: the latest story in a few lines, a model of where GDP growth is heading, and charts on growth, spending, jobs, inflation, energy, rates, credit and the federal budget. Everything refreshes from public data as new numbers are released.

Recession

All data comes from FRED at the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, which republishes figures from BEA, BLS, the Federal Reserve, Freddie Mac and the Treasury. Shaded bands mark NBER recessions. Hover over or tap a chart to read exact values.
